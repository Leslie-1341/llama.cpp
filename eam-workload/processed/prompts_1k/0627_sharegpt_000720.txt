Please read this about JMB: On aubades, his new album, blais writes for a chamber orchestra for the first time. This new evolution was first heard on his recently released piece, ‘murmures.’

In this show, he is joined by a three-person string ensemble to demonstrate the new music that has blossomed from his new approach.

Their performance is part of our Purcell Sessions, which puts an emphasis on those artists making bold steps forward in their work.

jean-michel blais is a pianist, composer and performer based in Montreal.

Toronto-based label Arts & Crafts released his first album, Il, written after two years of daily improvisation. It was named one of 2016’s top ten albums by Time magazine.

He has since collaborated with CFCF, blending electronics and piano on the Cascades EP, and released his sophomore album Dans ma main.

In 2019, he signed internationally with London-based record label Mercury KX (Universal / Decca) and launched Matthias & Maxime, the soundtrack of the eponymous film directed by Xavier Dolan.

For this, blais received an honorable gold record from the Cannes Soundtrack Award.
