You set up a hosting service company. Here's what companies need to do: Hire employees. Assign the role of the person responsible for each task.

1. Service Improvement: To improve the quality and stability of existing hosted services by identifying and resolving customer complaints and feedback.
2. New Service Planning: Planning and launching new hosting services that meet the needs of the target market and align with the company's goals.
3. Establishment and review of policies: creation of guidelines and rules for service provision, regular review
4. Roadmap management: strategies, goals and schedules for service development and improvement, etc.
Roadmap establishment and management
5. Service QA: Develop and implement testing and review procedures, monitor service performance to ensure quality of service delivery
6. Operation of the Service: Managing inventory, ordering and collection related to the provision of the Service, ensuring adequate inventory and timely delivery, monitoring the performance of the Service and implementing improvements.
7. Affiliate Settlement: Issuance of tax invoice for checking affiliate sales data and cost settlement;
Guaranteed accuracy of data and timely payments
8. CS Management: Responding to customer inquiries and issues through various channels
9. Quotation Proposal: Establish and provide service quotations according to customer requirements
10. Technical Support: Assistance with technical issues related to websites, servers or other hosting services.
11. Revenue/Cost/ROI Management: Hosting Service Revenue Analysis, Cost Structure Management and Optimization
12. Data Visualization: Using Tableau software to provide visual representations of data
13. DB management: managing and maintaining databases used for service provision, ensuring data accuracy, security and accessibility
14. DB Operation Automated Management: Automating database-related tasks to increase efficiency and reduce errors, including backup, maintenance and data migration
15. Work Standardization Management: Work process standardization to ensure work consistency and quality
16. Establishment of work manuals and policies: Establishment of standard operation manuals, policies and documentation
17. External Audit Response: Preparing and responding to external audits to comply with relevant laws and regulations
18. Report Suspicion of Forgery of KISA Sending Number, Spam SMS: Monitoring and reporting of suspicious activity related to the service, including potential spam messages and change of sender number
19. UDB Reserved Word Management: Managing specific words to be used as company-specific domain names
20. Certificate Management: Managing and renewing security certificates for services to ensure secure communication between clients and servers.
