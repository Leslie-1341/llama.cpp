Tentatively I am planning on building a database for the geology of a suite of wells (on the order of a few hundred). Each well has a XY position, depths, and various samples, geologic descriptions, interpretations, measurements, etc. associated with those depths. Currently these are available in a series of spreadsheets, which makes comparing across those sheets difficult. I don't quite have a specific research question in mind, but this would make data exploration and analysis tasks much quicker/easier. For example, you could query all wells that pass through a specific limestone unit, returning the list of wells and the start and bottom depths of the layer. Similarly, if looking at the local geology around one well of interest, you could do a spatial query of neighboring wells, incorporating constraints based on sample availability, lithology, etc.
