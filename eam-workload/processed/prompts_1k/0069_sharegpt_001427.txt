One of the benefits (or detriments) of social proof is that people can gain (or feign) credibility through a number usually not related to capital. Unfortunately a few bad apples spoils the whole crop as there are a whole slew of individuals or businesses out there use money to gain social proof through clickfarms and botting. This skews the landscape to where the people with money are ultimately with the most control over markets, consumers, headlines, and trends.
