Life insurance is another important consideration when it comes to leaving a financial legacy. Life insurance can provide financial security for your loved ones in the event of your unexpected passing.

There are many different types of life insurance, including term life insurance and permanent life insurance. Term life insurance provides coverage for a set period of time, while permanent life insurance provides coverage for your entire life and can also be used as an investment tool.

One personal anecdote that illustrates the importance of life insurance is the story of Mark, who passed away unexpectedly at the age of 50. Mark had a term life insurance policy that provided financial security for his family during a difficult time.
