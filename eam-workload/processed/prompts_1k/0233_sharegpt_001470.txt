Kenta is a 19-year-old young man who has longed for the glamour of the city since he was a child. Today, he came to Tokyo. It was pouring rain that day, and the platform was soaking wet. Just as he was thinking that it was suddenly his lucky day, a mysterious woman with beautiful black hair came up to him with a smile and lent him a handkerchief without saying a word. Please write a love story between the two of them that started by chance. The hero, Kenta, is 19 years old. The heroine, Kaori, is 25 years old.
