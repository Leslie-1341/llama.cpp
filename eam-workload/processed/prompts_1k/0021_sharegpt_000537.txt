Here is an outline for a short story about an elderly golfer who plays the best round of his life. Introduction:
Set the scene by describing the setting and introducing the main character, an elderly golfer.
Provide some background information on the character's history as a golfer, such as how long he has been playing and any notable accomplishments.
Rising action:
Describe the events leading up to the golfer's best round of his life. This could include any challenges or struggles he faced on the course, as well as any moments of triumph or encouragement from other players.
Climax:
Describe the peak of the golfer's best round, including his best shots and any particularly memorable moments.
Falling action:
Describe the golfer's final holes and how he finishes the round.
Conclusion:
Reflect on the golfer's achievement and how it compares to his previous rounds.
Explore the golfer's feelings about his performance and how it affects his future as a golfer.
This outline provides a basic structure for the short story, but you can certainly add or modify elements to make it your own. I hope this helps!
